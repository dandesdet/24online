Fully editable. Tweak copy, swap images, adjust layouts, or regenerate any part until it matches your brand and requirements.
How accurate is the marketplace formatting?
Listings are built to each marketplace's spec — image ratios, character limits, A+ modules, and category rules — so they're ready to upload without manual reformatting.

Just a product photo and a few basics — name, key features, and target marketplace. The pipeline handles the rest, from copy to gallery to A+ content.

Yes. Your uploads, brand kits, and generated assets stay tied to your account and aren't shared or used to train public models.
